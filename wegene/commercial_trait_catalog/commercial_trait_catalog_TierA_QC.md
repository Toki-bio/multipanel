5. **Consumer-friendly names** should use probabilistic language (e.g., 'association', 'tendency', 'pattern') rather than definitive language (e.g., 'requirement', 'metabolism').
6. **Report language** must include explicit caveats that results are probabilistic, not deterministic, and externally unvalidated.
